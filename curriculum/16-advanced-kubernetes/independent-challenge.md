# Independent Challenge

> **Module:** [16 — Advanced Kubernetes](README.md) · Previous: [Final production-readiness assessment](production-readiness-assessment.md) · Next: [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md)

Propose one advanced capability for CloudCart: an operator, mesh, policy engine,
eBPF data plane, event-driven scaler, or fleet manager. Start with the unmet
requirement and compare it with a simpler implementation.

Deliver an API/ownership design, privileges and threat model, reconciliation or
traffic flow, resource/cost budget, availability and upgrade model, observability,
failure injection, backup/recovery effect, migration/exit plan, and ADR. Build a
small isolated proof that demonstrates one benefit and one controlled failure.

Acceptance requires measurable evidence, least privilege, precise cleanup, and
an explicit decision to adopt, defer, or reject. Product installation alone is
not a passing outcome.
