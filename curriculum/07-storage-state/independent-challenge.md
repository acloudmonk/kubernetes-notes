# Independent Challenge

> **Module:** [07 — Kubernetes Storage and State](README.md) · Previous: [Lab 2 — Break, recover, back up, and restore](labs/02-break-recover-restore.md) · Next: [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md)

Deploy a stateful application other than CloudCart with a headless governing
Service, a separate client Service, a StatefulSet, and dynamically provisioned
storage. Do not copy the module manifests or claim HA without replication.

Submit versioned manifests and evidence for provisioning/binding, stable DNS and
ordinal identity, Pod replacement with data retention, one Pending or mount
failure diagnosed from events, backup to an ignored/off-worktree artifact,
isolated restore, application-level validation, and precise cleanup. Include a
deletion matrix, failure-domain map, RPO/RTO, and a short managed-service versus
in-cluster decision.
